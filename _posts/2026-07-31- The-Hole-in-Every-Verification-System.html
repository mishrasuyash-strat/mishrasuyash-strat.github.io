The Hole in Every Verification System

Reasoning across contexts is path integration. Whether the answer depends on the path is a question about cohomology — and the part you cannot check is the part that gets exploited.

Suyash Mishra · July 2026
Code, papers and an interactive instrument accompany this note.

Here is a small failure that turns out to have a large shape.

Ask an AI agent whether a drug target matters in naïve CD4+ αβ T cells, and somewhere in a long chain of tool calls the question quietly becomes about CD4+ T cells. Nothing hallucinated. No step was wrong. The agent simply answered a coarser question than the one it was asked, and no diagnostic available at the coarser level can tell you it happened. The recent Medea system names this failure precisely and builds an impressive architecture against it: verify the cell type is representable in the chosen tool, check parameters before execution, audit provenance after, screen the literature for contextual relevance, and let a panel of agents deliberate and abstain when they disagree.

It is good engineering. This note is about a class of failure it cannot catch, no matter how well it is implemented — and about what happens to an evidence ecosystem when you deploy a control that works on two out of three failure modes.

01Reasoning across contexts is path integration

Start somewhere concrete. Suppose you know an effect in one setting and want it in another. You do not have a direct study, so you chain: the effect in primary care, bridged to specialist care, bridged to hospital. Each bridge is a comparison estimated on a population the two settings share.

Formalise it. Let contexts be the nodes of a graph, and put an edge between two contexts whenever there is a population on which they can be compared — the nerve of a covering of context space. Evidence is then a function $\omega$ on edges: $\omega_{ij}$ is the measured contrast between contexts $i$ and $j$. A claim assignment is a function $\theta$ on nodes. Evidence is coherent with a claim when

$$\omega_{ij} \;=\; \theta_j - \theta_i \qquad\text{for every edge } ij. \label{eq:exact}$$

An agent asked for the claim at target $t$, anchored at $a$, walks a path $\gamma$ and adds up what it finds:

$$\hat\theta^{\,\gamma}_t \;=\; \theta_a + \sum_{e\in\gamma}\pm\,\omega_e \;=\; \theta_a + \langle\omega,\gamma\rangle .$$

That is a line integral. Which raises the obvious question.

02When does the answer depend on the route?

Take two paths $\gamma$ and $\gamma'$ between the same endpoints. Their difference $z=\gamma-\gamma'$ is a closed loop, and

$$\hat\theta^{\,\gamma}_t-\hat\theta^{\,\gamma'}_t \;=\; \langle\omega,\,z\rangle .$$

If $\omega$ satisfies \eqref{eq:exact} for some $\theta$ — if it is a coboundary, in the language of cochain complexes — then it pairs to zero with every loop, because $\langle\delta^0 x, z\rangle = \langle x, \partial z\rangle = 0$. Every path gives the same answer. If it is not, the discrepancy around a loop is nonzero, and it depends on $\omega$ only through its class in the first cohomology group $H^1$.

The first thing worth internalising

An agent's transported conclusion is independent of its reasoning path if and only if the evidence cochain is exact. Disagreement between two valid reasoning routes is not noise. It is the holonomy of a cohomology class.

This is not a new phenomenon dressed in new notation. Evidence synthesis has tested for it for twenty years under the name loop inconsistency: direct and indirect evidence around a closed loop of treatment comparisons can each be individually sound and jointly incoherent [2]. What the topological framing adds is a decomposition, an impossibility result, and — as we will see — a distributional theory.

03Three kinds of disagreement, and only one of them matters

The space of evidence fields splits orthogonally into three pieces. This is the discrete Hodge decomposition, familiar from statistical ranking [5]:

$$\omega \;=\; \underbrace{g}_{\text{gradient}} \;+\; \underbrace{c}_{\text{curl}} \;+\; \underbrace{h}_{\text{harmonic}} .$$

Each piece has an operational meaning, and they are genuinely different problems wearing the same clothes.

ComponentWhat it isWhat to do
Gradient Per-context calibration offsets — one lab reads high, one registry undercounts Recalibrate against an anchor and proceed
Curl Local logical inconsistency, visible where three contexts overlap Re-measure, or let the panel argue it out
Harmonic Structural non-transportability. The covering is too coarse Abstain, and refine the covering

04The impossibility result

Now look at what a verification check actually is. Context representability: a statement about one node. Parameter compatibility: one node. Bridging plausibility: one edge. Triple-overlap coherence: one triangle. Provenance audit of an execution: one node. Every check in the architecture is supported on a single simplex of the nerve.

And here is the problem. The harmonic component satisfies $\delta^1 h = 0$, so it passes every triangle check. It is orthogonal to every coboundary, so no recalibration removes it. Yet by the result in §02 it produces nonzero disagreement between valid reasoning paths.

The result

Any verification scheme whose checks are supported on simplices of the nerve cannot distinguish $\omega$ from $\omega+h$ for any harmonic $h$. Detecting the obstruction requires a statistic supported on a cycle basis — a genuinely global computation. The gap is structural, not a matter of implementation quality.

You can pass every check at every step and return an answer that is an artefact of your route through the evidence.

05Does gating on the right thing actually help?

A decomposition is only worth the trouble if acting on it beats the obvious alternative. The obvious alternative — the thing a careful engineer builds — is to measure total disagreement and abstain when it is large. So we held the estimator fixed and changed only the abstention statistic.

Across 2,400 simulated transport problems in two structurally different domains — a pharma real-world-evidence site indexed by CKD stage × care setting, and a consumer credit site indexed by region × macroeconomic regime, where the regime axis is a circle and therefore carries obstruction by construction — the discriminating result is this. Harmonic energy predicts the error that survives optimal estimation. So — as it turns out — does curl energy, and the reason why is the subject of §06, which is the most interesting thing in this note and the one place my first draft was simply wrong.

What does that buy in units a business recognises? Take a probability-of-default model inventory spanning five retail segments and four macroeconomic regimes. Its degrees of freedom partition as 42 comparisons = 19 calibration + 6 coherence + 17 transport. Seventeen independent loops can carry an incoherent story that no pairwise or triple validation will detect — and you know that from the governance architecture, before examining a single number.

06Where the obstruction actually comes from — and a claim I had to withdraw

My first version of this work injected the harmonic component into the simulation and then reported, with some satisfaction, that it predicted error. A colleague pointed out the obvious: that is assuming the conclusion. So I removed the injection entirely and asked where holonomy comes from if you refuse to put it there by hand.

The answer is clean enough that it should probably have been the starting point. Let each coarse context be a union of fine strata, with true effect

$$\theta_{c,s} \;=\; \mu_c \;+\; \lambda_s \;+\; m\,\gamma_{c,s},$$

where $\gamma$ is a context-by-stratum interaction — effect modification — and $m$ its strength. Any overlap between two contexts can only estimate the contrast on the case mix it actually contains, so the bridging evidence is $\omega_{ij}=\sum_s w_{ij,s}(\theta_{j,s}-\theta_{i,s})$.

Now set $m=0$. Then $\omega_{ij}=\mu_j-\mu_i$ regardless of the mixture, the cochain is exact, and there is no obstruction. Turn $m$ up and the contrast starts to depend on which strata that particular overlap happened to sample — and the loop stops closing.

The mechanism

Non-transportability is effect modification combined with overlap-specific population composition. Simpson's paradox on a network rather than in a single table. At zero effect modification the harmonic energy is $1.5\times10^{-15}$ — machine zero, not merely small.

That is a much better paper than the one I had. But it cost me a result.

The withdrawal

I had claimed that curl energy carries no information about irreducible error — that only the harmonic component matters, and that an engineer gating on total conflict is therefore using a diluted signal. Under the honest model:

Curl is nearly as predictive as harmonic. My earlier result was an artefact of drawing the two components independently; in a mechanistic model one latent cause generates both, so of course they co-vary. What survives is narrower and, I think, more defensible. Harmonic energy retains independent predictive value after conditioning. And the triage is untouched, because it was never a claim about prediction — it was a claim about remedy. Curl is repaired by re-measuring the triple. Harmonic is not repairable at that granularity by any amount of data whatsoever. Two quantities can both predict trouble while demanding entirely different responses, and it is the response the decomposition is for.

Two more corrections, since we are here

"Exact" was overclaimed. The $F$-test is exact under isotropic noise, and real bridging estimates differ in precision. I had assumed ignoring this would be conservative. It is not: at a fourfold spread of edge precisions the empirical size is 0.071 against a nominal 0.05, a 42% inflation of the false-positive rate. The fix is a change of metric — whiten by precision with $D=\operatorname{diag}(1/\sigma_e)$, sending $\omega\mapsto D\omega$, $\delta^0\mapsto D\delta^0$, $\delta^1\mapsto\delta^1 D^{-1}$. Since $\delta^1 D^{-1}D\delta^0=\delta^1\delta^0=0$, the whitened complex is a genuine cochain complex and every result carries over unchanged. Size falls to 0.061 and power rises.

Missing data hides obstruction rather than manufacturing it. I had listed the confounding of evidence gaps with genuine incoherence as a limitation, assuming gaps would inflate $\dim H^1$. The sign is the other way. Deleting an overlap destroys cycles faster than it destroys the triangles that fill them, so on a site with true $\dim H^1 = 21$, the observed value falls to 12.0 at 30% missing overlaps.

The dangerous direction

$\dim H^1$ computed on an incomplete nerve is a lower bound on the obstruction. A sparse evidence base looks more coherent than it is — which means the prevalence figures in the inconsistency literature, already acknowledged there as underpowered, are understatements for a second and structural reason.

07A test, and a domain where the null is exact

A negative result is more useful with a constructive companion. Here the degrees of freedom of an evidence network partition exactly:

$$\underbrace{|E|}_{\text{comparisons}} \;=\; \underbrace{(|V|-k)}_{\text{calibration}} \;+\; \underbrace{\operatorname{rank}\delta^1}_{\text{coherence}} \;+\; \underbrace{\beta_1}_{\textsc{transport}} .$$

Under the null that a coherent global claim exists, observed with homoscedastic noise, the harmonic and curl energies are independent $\chi^2$ variates on their respective dimensions. So

$$F \;=\; \frac{\|h\|^2/\beta_1}{\|c\|^2/\operatorname{rank}\delta^1} \;\sim\; F\!\left(\beta_1,\operatorname{rank}\delta^1\right)$$

is an exact test that a global claim exists. It is an analysis of variance whose residual is partitioned by topology rather than by design factors. Over 4,000 null replications it is correctly sized — 0.047 at nominal 0.05, Kolmogorov–Smirnov $p=0.135$ against uniformity — and reaches 80% power at a displacement of roughly 0.47 standard units.

Biology cannot falsify this cleanly, because the null — a coherent global claim exists — is never known independently of the data. Finance can. In an arbitrage-free market the log-quote cochain is exactly a coboundary, since $\log S_{ij}=v_j-v_i$ for a numeraire value $v$. Triangular arbitrage is the curl component. Loop arbitrage, on cycles that no simultaneously executable triangle fills, is the harmonic component.

A sentence I did not expect to write

Arbitrage is holonomy. And non-transportability is arbitrage in the space of claims. Both are certificates that your model of the world is incomplete, and both are worth exactly what it costs to close them.

Two things fall out of building the quote network properly. The G10 complex has $\dim H^1 = 0$: every cycle is filled by an executable triangle, which is a proof that standard triangular-arbitrage monitoring is complete there. Standard practice vindicated, and now for a stated reason. But add a deliverable-restricted tier whose regional crosses print in a fixing window disjoint from their USD legs, and a naive audit still reports zero while the executability-aware nerve reveals five independent loops carrying P&L that no triangle check can see.

08What survives deliberation

The natural objection is that this is what debate is for. Let the panel argue; disagreement will wash out.

It will not, and we can say precisely why. Model reconciliation as relaxation on the conflict energy. The Hodge Laplacian $L_1=\delta^0(\delta^0)^{\!\top}+(\delta^1)^{\!\top}\delta^1$ has kernel exactly the harmonic space, so under $\omega(t)=e^{-tL_1}\omega(0)$ the gradient and curl components decay exponentially and the harmonic component is conserved:

$$\lim_{t\to\infty}\|\omega(t)\|^2 \;=\; \|h\|^2 .$$

In simulation, over forty deliberation rounds, panel disagreement falls 39.7% and then stops — at a floor set by holonomy, not by noise. Accuracy does not improve: slope $-4.4\times10^{-4}$ per round, $p=0.17$. Harmonic energy moves by 0.00%.

This is a mechanism for a result the multi-agent literature has arrived at independently: debate under shared inputs behaves as a martingale [3], and closed-system deliberation is a Markov chain whose mutual information with the truth cannot increase. Those results say what deliberation cannot do. The conservation law says what is left over, and names it.

The practical warning. Consensus rises as the diagnostic falls. A panel that has deliberated to agreement has destroyed the only cheap signal it had. Conversely — and this is the most immediately usable finding in the whole programme — the entropy of conclusions sampled across distinct reasoning paths tracks harmonic energy at $\rho=0.959$, and is uncorrelated with curl ($p=0.06$) and gradient ($p=0.92$). A deliberation panel is already an obstruction detector. It just throws the signal away by averaging.

09The blind spot is a niche

So far this is a story about accidents. Now suppose the evidence has authors with preferences.

Perturbations decompose into the same three channels, and no single control covers them. Gradient manipulation shifts conclusions but is revealed by anchoring. Curl manipulation is revealed at triple overlaps. Harmonic manipulation moves a chaining agent's conclusion and is invisible to both. So $\dim H^1$ is not merely a diagnostic — it is the bandwidth available to an undetectable manipulator.

Which sets up the question. What happens to a population of evidence producers under a control that catches two channels out of three?

Biologists have a name for this. In infection dynamics it is competitive release: clearing susceptible parasites releases resistant ones into the vacated niche and accelerates their spread, and a more aggressive regimen can select harder for resistance for no detectable clinical gain [4]. What we have is the verification-architecture instance of the same mechanism. A partial control does not leave a gap. It concentrates the population into the gap.

10Power, not presence

The last step closes the loop, and it is the part I find most useful.

Detection should not be a free parameter in a game. So take the measured power function of the transport test and let a manipulator best-respond to it, choosing the displacement $\eta$ that maximises expected payoff against the probability of being caught.

The attacker does not attack as hard as it can. It picks the largest displacement that stays under the verifier's power curve. So improving a test shrinks the attack, not merely its success rate. Sanctions reduce the yield of manipulation; power reduces its size. Those are different levers, and the framework prices them in the same units.

Does any of this touch reality? One calibration point. A Cochrane methodology review across 75 papers and 4,583 studies finds industry-sponsored studies report favourable efficacy results at a risk ratio of 1.27 — and states the effect is attributable to biases that cannot be explained by standard risk-of-bias assessment tools [1]. A bias that survives an instrument is a component orthogonal to that instrument's span, and risk-of-bias tools are item-level checks on individual studies: maximally local.

11What this changes on Monday

Four things you can do without adopting any of the machinery.

Keep the disagreement. If you run a deliberation panel, log the spread of conclusions across reasoning routes and threshold on it. You are already computing the best cheap estimator of the obstruction; do not average it away.

Audit the architecture before the data. The degrees-of-freedom partition is computable from your evidence network alone. A model inventory over five segments and four macroeconomic regimes decomposes as 42 comparisons = 19 calibration + 6 coherence + 17 transport. Seventeen loops can carry an incoherent story that no pairwise or triple validation will detect — and you know that before examining a single number.

Treat abstention as an instruction. A nonzero class does not just warn. It names the loop, and therefore names the stratification whose absence caused the problem. Under GxP or SR 11-7, an abstention that arrives with a cycle witness is a materially different artefact from a confidence score.

Buy power, not just punishment. Since $\operatorname{rank}\delta^1$ is a matroid rank function, greedy allocation of a study budget on benefit-per-cost is provably optimal — and it matters: at a fixed budget, greedy left 24 open channels where random allocation left 29.

Try it

An interactive instrument accompanies this note. Build a context site from two attributes, delete overlaps to represent evidence gaps, and click cells to commission joint-validation studies. The degrees-of-freedom partition, the Hodge energies, the exact $F$-test, the attacker's best response and the evolutionary verdict all recompute live from the topology. Closing one cell can flip the terminal readout from INVADED to STABLE. That dependency is the entire argument, and it seemed better to let you push on it than to ask you to take it on trust.

12What would prove this wrong

I would rather name the falsifiers than defend the result.

The strongest test: in an evidence corpus with strategic producers — a regulatory filing archive, a registered-trial network — the distribution of detected manipulation should be depleted in the channels the deployed controls cover and enriched in the harmonic channel. If it is flat, the evolutionary argument is wrong where it matters.

The cheapest test: on real agent traces, entropy of conclusions across deliberation rounds should correlate with harmonic energy on the corresponding nerve, and not with the other two components. Every asset needed for that experiment is already public — the Medea repository, its benchmarks, and the released yeast genetic-interaction screen. If it fails, the practical estimator is worthless and only the expensive construction survives.

And the honest caveats, in one place. There is no real data in any of this. The simulations are controlled validations of a theory, and while §06 removes the circularity by letting holonomy emerge from a mechanism rather than injecting it, an emergent mechanism in a model I wrote is still a model I wrote. Correlated errors across overlaps remain unhandled — whitening fixes unequal variances, not covariance. And eliciting the right context factorisation in the first place is the hard applied problem, on which none of this has anything to say.

The fastest real test needs no new collection at all. Published network meta-analyses report contrast estimates and their standard errors; reconstruct the nerve from a published network, apply the whitened test, and compare against the node-splitting results the original authors reported. That puts this framework directly against the established method on its own ground, using only numbers already in print. Until that exists, this is theory with simulation support, and it should be read that way.

The thing I keep coming back to is how ordinary the starting point was. An agent answered a slightly coarser question than the one it was asked. Follow that carefully enough and you arrive at a conserved quantity, an exact test, a reason standard FX practice is correct, and an evolutionary prediction about what happens when you deploy a control that almost works. Structure was there the whole time. It usually is.

And the second thing: the most useful hour I spent on this was the one where I took the simulation apart because someone said it assumed its own conclusion. The result I lost was real, and the paper is better without it. If you take one thing from this note, let it be that rather than the cohomology.
